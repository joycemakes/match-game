# Surgery sounds

Screams for the Looksmaxxer's surgery scenes (added 3 November 2026). All of them are cut from CC0 (public domain) recordings on OpenGameArt.org, so no credit is required. They're credited here anyway.

| File | Cut from | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scream-1.mp3` to `scream-6.mp3` | "Male Grunt/Yelling sounds" (`yelling sounds.zip`: 3yell16, 3yell13, 3yell6, yell11, 3yell9, yell10), https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds | HaelDB | CC0 (dual-licensed CC0 / OGA-BY 3.0; used under CC0) |
| `death-scream.mp3` | 3yell13 and 3yell6 from the pack above, then a groan from "grunts of male death and pain" (`death pain grunts.wav`, 10.0 to 12.35 s), https://opengameart.org/comment/3008 | HaelDB; thebardofblasphemy | CC0 |
| `groan.mp3` | "grunts of male death and pain" (`death pain grunts.wav`, 3.96 to 6.09 s) | thebardofblasphemy | CC0 |

Every clip was trimmed, converted to mono and loudness-normalized, then saved as 64 kbps MP3.

The drill, bone saw and clippers sounds are not files. The game makes them itself with Web Audio (`machineSound` in `preview.html`).
